01 · How the work connects

Clinic, health systems, policy, then AI and strategy.

I trained as a doctor, then moved into public health because many of the failures I saw in care were failures of financing, data and policy, not of clinical skill.

That led to health insurance and utilisation research in Rajasthan, policy analysis with Oxford, and graduate training in health economics and financing at LSE and LSHTM. Since 2025 I have applied the same evidence habits to clinical AI evaluation and health strategy work, from commercial due diligence to venture design.

I am looking for roles where that mix is the job: health strategy and consulting, health tech, and clinical AI.
